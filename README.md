# 9th Study Project

A student-friendly Grade 9 FBISE study companion. The first version is a lightweight, mobile-friendly static web app: no account, no build step, and no framework.

## Run it
Open `index.html` in a browser, or enable GitHub Pages: Settings → Pages → Deploy from a branch → `main` → `/ (root)`.

## Included
- Home dashboard and subject selector
- Starter important-question bank with answer guidance
- Official FBISE model-paper/document viewers
- Previous-paper archive viewer
- High Achiever Papers viewer for real student answer books
- Research tab with verified findings, scheme warnings, source register and limitations

The app's Model Papers section now includes in-app viewers for the revised scheme, grading notification, SLO resource portal, practical resources, e-sheet/OMR samples and rubric archive.

## Research integrity
- FBISE distinguishes the Inclusive Scheme of Studies 2024 from the older Scheme of Studies 2006.
- The FBISE curriculum/model-paper page lists Grade IX 50% MCQ / 50% descriptive frameworks for Biology, Chemistry, Computer Science, Mathematics and Physics.
- FBISE notification dated 16 November 2026 says Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry and Biology/Computer Science papers will be bilingual from First Annual Examination 2027.
- Inclusive Scheme of Studies 2024 was implemented for SSC-I from academic session 2025 and SSC-II from academic session 2026. Students continuing under Scheme 2006 remain under that scheme for their permitted certification chances.
- Under Scheme 2024, Pakistan Studies is allocated at SSC-II; Grade IX subject combinations and allocations differ from the old scheme.
- The app does not call questions statistically repeated until a full paper-by-paper comparison has been completed.

## Limitations
- Official archive pages are shown inside embedded viewers; browser security headers may prevent some embeds.
- High Achiever Papers are real marked answer-book scans, but each script needs individual inspection before describing answer quality. The board listing does not publish student marks or identify each script as a full-mark paper.
- The question bank is a starter set of concept-practice prompts, not a statistically verified repeated-question list or a prediction of the next paper.
- FBISE may update documents. Recheck the official board before an examination.
